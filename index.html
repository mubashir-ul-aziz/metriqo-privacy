Privacy Policy

Metriqo – Invoice Maker
Last updated: 29 October 2026
Developer: Mubashir ul Aziz

This policy explains how Metriqo’s Android app handles your information.

1. Your information

Metriqo stores your business details, logo, customers, items, invoices, payments and settings on your device. The developer does not receive or store this business data on a server.

You do not need to create a Metriqo account. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools.

You are responsible for having permission to store and use your customers’ personal information.

2. Subscriptions

Paid subscriptions use Google Play for payments and RevenueCat to manage subscription access.

These services process purchase records and information needed to verify your plan. RevenueCat also receives a random app identifier and basic device and app information. Metriqo does not send your invoices, customers or business records to RevenueCat.

The developer does not receive your payment card details.

Subscription status is saved on your device for offline use. Paid access requires an online subscription check at least every 24 hours.

For more information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

3. Backups and sharing

You can export your business data as a backup file and restore it later. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe. Restoring a backup replaces the business data currently in the app.

When you share, email or print an invoice, business card or backup, the selected app or service receives that information. Its own privacy policy applies. The developer does not receive a copy.

Google Drive and cloud backup are currently unavailable. This policy will be updated before those features become active.

4. Device access and security

Metriqo uses internet access for subscription services. It accesses a logo image or backup file when you select one using your device’s picker.

Optional App Lock uses your device’s authentication system. Metriqo does not receive or store your fingerprint or face data.

The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or SMS.

App data is kept in private device storage, and subscription connections use HTTPS. No storage method is completely secure. Protect your device and exported backups.

5. Keeping and deleting data

Your local data remains until you delete it, clear the app’s storage or uninstall Metriqo. You can view, edit and export your records in the app.

Copies saved outside the app, including shared files and exported backups, must be deleted separately. Google and RevenueCat retain purchase information under their own policies. Contact the developer about requests concerning subscription data.

Uninstalling Metriqo does not cancel your subscription. Manage or cancel it through Google Play.

6. Your choices

The developer does not sell your information or share it with advertisers. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights. Contact the developer for questions or requests.

7. Policy updates

This policy may change as Metriqo develops. Updates will appear on this page with a revised date.
